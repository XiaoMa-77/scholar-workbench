## 1. Data-Driven Vision-Language Models for Remote Sensing: A Survey（12 分）

- **作者**：Yue Zhou, Shujun Zhao, Xue Yang, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31304v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.LG
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：During the deep learning era, innovations in remote sensing (RS) vision models primarily focused on optimizing network architectures for specific tasks and conducting end-to-end training. However, with the advent of the Vision-Language Models (VLMs) era, constrained by substantial computational resource demands, the mainstream research paradigm has shifted towards adapting general-purpose VLMs using RS vertical domain data for fine-tuning, rather than modifying their core architectures. This transition marks a change in the innovation pathway from model-centric to data-centric. Despite the increasing importance of data, the academic community still lacks a systematic, data-centric survey to guide researchers in constructing high-quality RS vision-language datasets for various application scenarios and in conducting effective model fine-tuning and performance evaluation based on these datasets. To address this gap, this paper presents a systematic review of the development status of RS datasets tailored for VLMs. It further provides a comprehensive practical guide for RS researchers in the large model era, covering the entire pipeline from data construction to fine-tuning and evaluation. This paper aims to encourage the academic community to build more diverse and higher-quality vision-language datasets for specialized RS sub-fields. Ultimately, the goal is to converge these efforts into a comprehensive RS vision-language corpus that offers extensive task coverage and a wide geographical distribution, thereby laying a solid data foundation for advancing towards general artificial intelligence in RS.

## 2. RankAlign: Vision-Language Representation Alignment via Rank Transformation（12 分）

- **作者**：Enzhe Zhao, Marco Fiorucci, Lamberto Ballan
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31145v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：While the Platonic Representation Hypothesis posits that vision and language models converge toward compatible latent geometries, recovering class-level correspondences between unpaired modalities without cross-modal supervision remains challenging. Existing relational matching methods compare within-modality similarity kernels directly, although the same semantic structure may be represented through different numerical parameterizations across modalities. We propose RankAlign, a rank-based normalization strategy for class-level unpaired vision-language matching. RankAlign transforms each within-modality similarity kernel into an ordinal relational representation before permutation matching, while leaving the correspondence space and matching solver unchanged. The resulting representation is invariant to strictly increasing reparameterizations of similarity values while preserving the ordinal structure used for matching. We provide exact-recovery and stability guarantees that characterize correspondence recovery under both exact and partial ordinal agreement. Experiments across diverse frozen vision and language encoders and multiple datasets demonstrate improved and more stable class-level correspondence recovery, including under label corruption and domain shift.

## 3. RECAP: Looking Once Is Not Enough for Vision-Language Reasoning（12 分）

- **作者**：Zhaolu Kang, Tailong Luo, Chenxin Li, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30834v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Long chain-of-thought reasoning improves vision-language models (VLMs), but it also exposes a temporal grounding failure: as generation unfolds, models may drift from image evidence while reinforcement learning still provides only a final-answer reward. We study this gap through visual revisit, a temporally localized reactivation of image evidence during reasoning. Across three VLM backbones, revisit peaks are predictive of correctness and causally linked to performance: masking them degrades accuracy more than masking non-peaks, while injecting revisit-like peaks into failed rollouts partially restores correct answers. Control analyses show that this signal is not explained by raw attention strength, uncertainty, logit margin, or hidden-state norm. We propose RECAP, a GRPO-compatible training method that turns detached revisit traces into a credit-assignment signal. RECAP uses revisit-conditioned advantage estimation, dynamic discounting, and gated value prediction to propagate final rewards through visually important reasoning steps, without extra rewards, additional rollouts, or inference-time changes. Across Qwen2.5-VL-7B, InternVL3-8B, and LLaVA-OV-7B, RECAP consistently improves over GRPO and VPPO, yielding $+6.1$--$+6.5$ HallusionBench gains over the SFT base while preserving reasoning and text-only performance. Results on larger LoRA-tuned models, Chinese zero-shot benchmarks, visual perturbations, and human evaluation further demonstrate robust improvements in image-grounded reasoning.

## 4. Loop to Look Better: Learning Adaptive Depth for Question-Guided Perception in Vision-Language Models（12 分）

- **作者**：Wencheng Ye, Anning Hu, Hongye Fang, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30824v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Visual perception should be iterative and task-directed, with representations refined in response to the question and the need for further processing. While Vision-Language Models (VLMs) increasingly support extended reasoning, their visual tokens are still typically acquired in a single, task-agnostic encoder pass. This creates an evidence-acquisition bottleneck: models may reason longer without refining the visual evidence needed to answer the question. We propose LoopLook, a lightweight encoder-side recurrent perception framework that equips pretrained VLMs with adaptive visual depth. LoopLook recurrently revisits a middle-to-late window of the vision encoder, injecting question representations at each loop to guide visual refinement. A learned stopping mechanism adaptively determines the recurrent depth, while a token-wise Mixture-of-Looks (MoL) combines representations from different loops, allowing each visual token to selectively integrate information acquired across recurrent steps. Across four backbones and eight benchmarks, LoopLook consistently improves performance over the baseline, yielding gains of up to 8.6% on MMStar and 8.1% on HallusionBench. Our analysis shows that LoopLook progressively refines visual attention and adaptively allocates depth according to task difficulty. It also better preserves task-relevant visual information under reduced input resolution, while increasing time to first token (TTFT) by only 5.1-6.5%. By bringing recurrence from reasoning back into perception, LoopLook transforms visual perception from a one-shot glimpse into an adaptive process of recurrent refinement.

## 5. WorldForge V2: Eliciting Camera-Trajectory Alignment in Video Diffusion Models（9 分）

- **作者**：Chenxi Song, Yanming Yang, Tong Zhao, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31640v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Video diffusion models have learned rich 3D/4D priors, making them promising for scene generation and video re-rendering. However, their use in spatial tasks is limited by imprecise camera control, spatial and temporal inconsistencies, and entangled camera motion with scene dynamics. Existing approaches, such as task-specific fine-tuning and post-process warping, often introduce visual artifacts, generalize poorly, or incur high computational costs. We propose WorldForge V2 to achieve accurate camera control while preserving the generative capabilities of pretrained video models. We first develop a training-free guidance framework using warped references to iteratively refine generation along a target camera path. Optical-flow analysis identifies motion-related latent channels for selective guidance, helping separate motion control from appearance. A dual-path strategy further compares guided and unguided predictions to correct artifacts from imperfect references. Together, these components guide viewpoint changes while preserving scene appearance and object motion. To reduce refinement costs, v-cache approximates the correction using the preceding denoising prediction, avoiding an additional model evaluation. We further extend motion localization from latent channels to network blocks and introduce Trajectory LoRA, which concentrates adaptation on blocks most responsive to trajectory guidance. A two-stage training strategy teaches these adapters to denoise the states encountered during guided generation, using limited supervision effectively. This enables reliable camera control with very little training data, with a single training scene already supporting generalization to unseen scenes. Quantitative and qualitative evaluations across multiple datasets show that our method achieves state-of-the-art generation quality and trajectory accuracy in camera-controlled video generation.

## 6. RaSFNet: Ray-aligned and Scan-aware Fusion Network for LiDAR Semantic Segmentation（9 分）

- **作者**：Chuang Chen
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31186v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.LG
- **命中**：segmentation（标题 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：LiDAR point cloud semantic segmentation is a fundamental component of 3D perception for autonomous driving and robotics. Projection-based methods map 3D point clouds into 2D representations for feature extraction using 2D networks. However, aggregating multiple points during projection can obscure geometric differences within each frustum, while local feature extraction limits the modeling of spatial relationships across scanning regions. Moreover, proximity in the projected representation does not necessarily imply proximity in 3D space, and interactions between point and projected features may mix features from different ranges, reducing semantic discriminability. To address these challenges, we propose the Ray-aligned and Scan-aware Fusion Network (RaSFNet), which incorporates point cloud geometry and LiDAR scanning characteristics into projection-based semantic segmentation. Specifically, the Ray-Aligned Geometric Distribution Encoding (RAGDE) module characterizes point distributions within each frustum using a ray-aligned coordinate system. To model spatial context, a Scan-Topology-Aware Mamba (STAM) block organizes frustum features according to the LiDAR azimuth sampling pattern and captures long-range dependencies across scanning regions. In addition, a Range-Constrained Point-Frustum Fusion (RCPFF) mechanism uses shared range information to guide feature exchange between point and frustum representations while maintaining spatial correspondence. RaSFNet achieves mIoU scores of 70.2% and 77.7% on the SemanticKITTI and nuScenes validation sets, respectively, indicating competitive segmentation performance compared with the evaluated methods.

## 7. KinoUAV: A Benchmark for Safe and Stable UAV Vision-Language Navigation with Kinodynamic Modeling（9 分）

- **作者**：Chenyao Yang, Peijin Wang, Yingchao Feng, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30609v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）
- **摘要**：Unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) have recently emerged as important remote sensing platforms, and UAV navigation has shown great potential in remote sensing applications. However, existing methods do not explicitly incorporate kinodynamic modeling or safety distances into trajectory generation, resulting in high absolute yaw rates, frequent collisions, and low navigation success rates, which limit safety and motion stability. To address this challenge, we introduce KinoUAV, a kinodynamic benchmark comprising a dataset and KinoUAV-Agent. First, we develop a hierarchical kinodynamic trajectory generation pipeline that drives UAV through acceleration actions. It uses waypoint chains from global planning and generates trajectories segment by segment under velocity, acceleration, and jerk constraints, reducing oscillations and improving trajectory stability. Furthermore, we introduce directional free-space distance (DFD) labels into the constructed dataset to describe the available clearance for safe motion decisions. The proposed KinoUAV-Agent predicts DFDs and uses them to generate safe actions, decoupling perception from action decision-making. This design enables autonomous obstacle avoidance and improves navigation safety. The benchmark incorporates the proposed Cruise-Seek task, in which the UAV first enters the target region and then searches for the target, reducing the difficulty of target search and improving navigation success. Experiments on KinoUAV show that KinoUAV-Agent outperforms existing methods, achieving the highest success rate while maintaining lower absolute yaw rates and collision rates. These results demonstrate the effectiveness of kinodynamic modeling for safe and stable UAV navigation.

## 8. Diffusion Language Models for Perplexity-Aware Black-Box Attacks（8 分）

- **作者**：Ali Al-Lawati, Elsayed Eshra, Dongwon Lee, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31558v1
- **分类**：cs.LG、cs.CL、stat.ML
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：\emph{Continuous-space} attacks find prompts that trigger malicious behaviors, such as overthinking, in black-box LLMs by sampling or optimizing over a low-dimensional Gaussian latent space and decoding it to text through a \emph{random mapping} and nearest-token lookup. Because the random mapping is not learned from language, its prompts can be highly unnatural, and as such easily detectable through input guardrails, and the risk estimates they yield are not grounded in natural prompts. In this paper, we demonstrate that the same continuous latent-space framework can be coupled directly to a learned generative distribution over language, a continuous diffusion language model (dLLM), which removes the artificial random mapping while preserving the standard-Gaussian latent formulation and the downstream sampling or optimization procedure. We evaluate this approach under two settings: sampling-based estimation using \emph{RareTrap} and optimization-based search using \emph{ThinkTrap}. We find that character-level perplexity drops from $10^3$--$10^7$ to 3--5 in RareTrap and from 20--28 to 4.3--5.4 in ThinkTrap. We also find that model risk estimates can differ by an order of magnitude across the two mappings.

## 9. Evaluation of Uncertainty-Aware Multi-Software Ensembles for Hippocampal Segmentation（8 分）

- **作者**：Gabriel Oliveira-Stahl, Anna Schroder, James Moggridge, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31505v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：segmentation（标题 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：Accurate hippocampal segmentation can be a useful tool for diagnosing and monitoring neurological conditions such as Alzheimer's disease and epilepsy. While numerous automated segmentation methods exist, their clinical adoption remains limited. Reliable uncertainty assessment can enhance trust and facilitate clinical translation. This study evaluates ensembles of five heterogeneous hippocampal segmentation methods - InnerEye, ASHS, FastSurfer, HippoSeg, and FreeSurfer - across two dementia datasets and one epilepsy dataset. The sub-ensemble containing InnerEye, FastSurfer, and HippoSeg emerged as both accurate and efficient, highlighting the feasibility of balancing computational cost and performance. Additionally, ensemble-derived uncertainty quantification with sample variance, mutual information, and predictive entropy is shown to reduce inaccurate segmentations by flagging low-confidence cases, potentially providing a mechanism for automatically escalating ambiguous cases for expert assessment. Lastly, this study explores the downstream clinical utility of ensemble-derived uncertainty, finding a significant correlation with motion artefact severity and a modest contribution to Alzheimer's disease classification.

## 10. Lightweight Frame Compensation for Real-Time Polyp Segmentation under Frame Skipping（8 分）

- **作者**：Weihao Gao
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31228v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：segmentation（标题 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：Real-time polyp segmentation in computer-assisted colonoscopy is hampered by the difficulty of deploying high-accuracy models under strict latency budgets. Frame skipping, which performs inference every K frames and reuses or interpolates the rest, improves throughput but introduces motion blur and lesion misalignment that degrade temporal consistency. To close this gap, we propose the Lightweight Frame Compensator (LFC), a 77K-parameter plug-and-play module inserted before a frozen segmentation backbone. Operating on a three-frame window, LFC fuses consecutive frames via spatio-temporal attention into a single compensated frame, and is trained only on pseudo-labels generated by a differently seeded model, without any expert annotation. Experiments on three polyp video benchmarks show that LFC recovers most of the accuracy degraded by direct frame skipping and, in several settings, reaches the accuracy of frame-by-frame inference, while retaining a 2.05X--2.55X throughput gain. We further evaluate the temporal stability of three backbones spanning CNN, pyramid Transformer, and global-attention Transformer architectures, revealing a spectrum of sensitivity to frame skipping that arises from how locally or globally each architecture aggregates misaligned input. The compensation gain of LFC follows this spectrum: it is largest for high-capacity Transformer backbones, which achieve the best static accuracy but suffer the most under skip-frame inference. LFC thus offers a lightweight, pluggable, and deployment-friendly route to real-time polyp segmentation.
