## 1. TimeLineXR: An Interactive Exploration of Time in Vision-Language Models（13 分）

- **作者**：Nidham Tekaya, Markus Passecker, Matthias Zeppelzauer
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37349v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Recently, it has been shown that vision-language models (VLMs) have a certain intrinsic understanding of time. They can estimate the temporal origin of objects and encode time along a low-dimensional, non-linear manifold inside their embedding space~\cite{tekaya2025matter}. That result is quantitative and reproducible, but the structure itself stays out of reach for end users: they cannot see it, query it with their own images, or inspect where a query lands relative to other years on the time axis. We present TimeLineXR, an interactive demonstration that turns this time axis into an explorable 3D scene. A web demo lets users upload or select images and see each placed on a color-coded 3D time axis; a Three.js WebXR scene renders the same time axis as a room-scale representation that can be grabbed, scaled, inspected, and queried by throwing image cards into it. Both clients run the same prediction pipeline against the same cached temporal index: encode the query with CLIP, project it through cosine-kernel KPCA, and snap it to the Bézier time axis. The system turns each image into a point on the time axis, so users can compare where different objects land and read off their estimated first year of appearance.Code source and reproduction steps can be found in: https://github.com/sterngefeuert/timeline-xr.

## 2. Vision-Language-Action or World-Action? Just Action Models（12 分）

- **作者**：Yonghao Yu, Lang Huang, Runyi Li, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37219v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.RO
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：The vision-language-action (VLA) and world-action model (WAM) communities debate whether control policies should inherit pretrained knowledge from vision-language models or from visual generators. The debate, however, bundles three separable choices: backbone initialization, the objective applied to future observations, and whether the machinery for that future loss is kept at inference. A gap between a VLA and a WAM therefore cannot simply be attributed to any one of them. We show that decoupling these choices yields a common framework for both families, which we term Just Action Model (JAM): a pretrained visual backbone with an action expert, trained with an action loss and a foresight loss. The foresight loss trains current-observation features to predict frozen encodings of the recorded future through a prediction head discarded after training, so no visual generation is involved in either training or inference, and the backbone can be chosen freely. We first compare objectives within fixed policy architectures, then evaluate the foresight recipe across backbone families. The resulting JAM policies outperform their matched FastWAM and ImageWAM counterparts on LIBERO and LIBERO-Plus, and achieve similar or slightly higher success rates on RoboTwin. Compared with FastWAM, JAM improves the success rates from 97.0%/55.3%/91.2% to 98.2%/64.5%/91.4%, respectively. With cached foresight targets, JAM reduces estimated online training FLOPs per sample per update by 73% relative to FastWAM. Our results show that future supervision improves control without visual generation or additional inference machinery, and strong foresight performance is attainable with either generator-initialized or vision-language backbones. Project Page: https://y0uroy.github.io/JAM

## 3. SplatScout: Query-Driven Part-Level Referring Segmentation in Gaussian Scenes（12 分）

- **作者**：Yihan Zang, Da Li, Shinkyu Park, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37098v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（摘要 ×3）；segmentation（标题 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Existing referring segmentation methods on Gaussian scenes primarily target whole objects, relying on segmenter-generated candidates from which a vision-language model (VLM) selects. Such predetermined fixed candidates may omit fine-grained parts needed as targets or cues for identifying objects. We introduce SplatScout, a query-driven, closed-loop approach that places perception under VLM control. The model actively selects views, directs segmentation, and inspects rendered 3D results to refine its predictions. For part-level evaluation, we introduce PartRef-ScanNet++, comprising 261 manually verified queries across 12 ScanNet++ scenes with mesh-derived ground truth. On PartRef-ScanNet++, SplatScout achieves 0.538 mIoU, substantially outperforming existing methods. Ablations demonstrate the benefits of iterative refinement, while comparisons across VLM backbones highlight the importance of effective tool-feedback utilization. SplatScout also achieves higher scores on four existing referring benchmarks. Code and the PartRef-ScanNet++ benchmark are available at https://github.com/kaust-violab/SplatScout.

## 4. V-LUMEN: Visual Lookup Memory for Embedding Scaling in Vision-Language Models（12 分）

- **作者**：Miso Choi, Daekeun Kim, Eunji Kim, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37019v1
- **分类**：cs.LG、cs.AI、cs.CL、cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Embedding-level scaling expands the capacity of language models through lookup-based memory rather than by increasing model depth, width, or dense computation. Extending this idea to vision-language models (VLMs) is challenging because visual features are continuous, spatially structured, and query-dependent, unlike discrete text tokens that naturally support n-gram lookup. We introduce V-LUMEN, a visual lookup memory for VLMs. V-LUMEN discretizes visual features with a frozen codebook, builds spatially aware memory keys through spatial aggregation-based hashing, and modulates them with text-conditioned relevance signals; all addressing is non-parametric, and the retrieved embeddings are fused into the backbone through a gated residual pathway. On five reasoning-intensive benchmarks, V-LUMEN substantially improves a frozen Qwen3-VL-2B backbone and transfers zero-shot to three benchmarks whose images are disjoint from the training data. At a matched parameter budget, moving part of a LoRA or MoE adapter's capacity into V-LUMEN preserves accuracy on two backbones, indicating that external visual memory is an independent capacity axis rather than a stronger adaptation method. Because lookup cost is constant in table size and incurred only once at prefill, this capacity grows in storage rather than in per-token compute. Code is available at https://github.com/miso-choi/V-Lumen.

## 5. LearnPrunerV2: Learnable Progressive Token Pruning for Vision-Language Models（12 分）

- **作者**：Rinyoichi Takezoe, Yaqian Li, Weiren Wang, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36986v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have demonstrated remarkable multimodal capabilities but suffer from prohibitive computational overhead due to the massive number of visual tokens. While token pruning is a promising solution, existing methods still suffer from two major drawbacks: 1) unreliable pruning criteria that fail to accurately localize task-specific regions due to inherent attention biases or query-agnostic designs, and 2) suboptimal pruning strategies that employ rigid, non-adaptive schedules, overlooking the dynamic evolution of token redundancy across different model depths. To address these challenges, we propose LearnPrunerV2, which extends the previous two-stage heuristic framework (LearnPruner) into a fully learnable architecture. Specifically, we integrate lightweight learnable modules into every layer of the VLM backbone, which estimate token importance by fusing intrinsic visual saliency with extrinsic query relevance captured via a shadow token. By uncovering a consistent staircase redundancy pattern across VLM layers, we further introduce Budget-Deterministic Pruning (BDP) to establish a hardware-friendly schedule with fixed per-layer budgets. Extensive experimental results across various benchmarks and VLMs demonstrate that LearnPrunerV2 consistently outperform previous state-of-the-art methods. Notably, LearnPrunerV2 preserves 99.8% of the original performance when pruning 77.8% of visual tokens on LLaVA-v1.5, achieving a superior accuracy-efficiency trade-off.

## 6. MRI-to-CT Translation of Spinal Images via Anatomy-Guided Pseudo-Pairing and Adversarial Diffusion（11 分）

- **作者**：Eduardo Nieto, Ibai Genua, Davide Scorza, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37134v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Magnetic Resonance Imaging (MRI) is routinely used to diagnose spinal disorders, but surgical planning often requires Computed Tomography (CT) for better visualization of vertebral bone anatomy. Synthesizing CT from MRI could avoid additional radiation exposure and streamline preoperative assessment. We propose a three-stage framework for spine MRI-to-CT synthesis. First, an anatomy-guided vertebral matching system identifies similar sagittal MRI and CT slices using spine segmentations and aligns them through rigid registration, creating pseudo-paired data from unpaired scans. Second, these pairs train an adversarial diffusion model for MRI-to-CT translation. Third, synthesized slices are assembled into 3D volumes and refined by a network that improves inter-slice continuity and structural coherence. The framework was trained on over 10,000 unpaired sagittal MRI and CT slices and evaluated on 1,911 slices from 83 MRI volumes spanning all spinal regions. Assessment combined distribution-based measures with vertebral segmentation metrics. Compared with diffusion alone, the proposed approach increased binary 3D vertebral Dice from 0.666 to 0.739. Volumetric post-processing reduced visible discontinuities between stacked slices and improved vertebral coherence in axial and coronal views. Combining anatomy-guided pseudo-pairing, adversarial diffusion and 3D refinement improves spine MRI-to-CT synthesis. Vertebral matching primarily enhanced anatomical alignment and fidelity, while volumetric refinement improved 3D consistency. These findings support further development of MRI-derived CT-like visualization for preoperative planning, particularly to preserve fine intensity details while maintaining volumetric coherence.

## 7. Look Only When Needed: Certificate-Guided Visual Routing for Efficient Diffusion VLMs（11 分）

- **作者**：Donghyeon Cho, Joowon Kim, Seungho Shin, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36671v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI、cs.LG
- **命中**：vision-language（摘要 ×3）；diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：Diffusion vision-language models (DVLMs) process their entire input at every denoising step. Since visual tokens make up 89-97% of this input on the benchmarks we study, most of the decoding computation is spent repeatedly processing the same image. Visual token pruning methods partially relieve this burden by removing part of the visual tokens, but they lose the removed information and still process the remaining image at every step. To measure how often the image is actually needed, we compare LLaDA-V's predictions with and without the image at every decoding step and find that removing the image changes a token being unmasked at only 22.8% of steps on average and at as few as 7.9%. We therefore propose LOWN, a decoding method that decides at every step whether to process the image and how many tokens to unmask. LOWN predicts the masked tokens without the image and processes the image only when this prediction disagrees with the last image-conditioned prediction. Agreement certifies the earlier prediction for reuse, and the number of certified tokens sets how many are unmasked in that step. We further observe that the need for the image differs across benchmarks, so LOWN's adaptive decision is needed rather than a fixed schedule. Without training or a tuned threshold, LOWN retains 99.6% of LLaDA-V's score on ten benchmarks at 35.3% of the decoding FLOPs on average. LOWN also generalizes across LLaDA-based DVLMs, composes orthogonally with visual token pruning, and runs under key-value caching for further efficiency gains. Code and project page are available at https://hyeon9698.github.io/LOWN/

## 8. GroundControl: Satellite-Conditioned Multi-View Diffusion for Reconstruction-Free Novel View Synthesis（9 分）

- **作者**：Akshay Krishnan, Matias Turkulainen, Jamie Wynn, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37545v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI、cs.LG
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Multi-view or video diffusion models have recently emerged as state-of-the-art approaches for novel-view synthesis from sparse image collections. While they can produce high quality images, they tend to hallucinate unrealistic spatial layouts when synthesizing views outside the input views (scene extrapolation). We propose to address this challenge for outdoor scenes by using satellite images as an additional information source to condition the multi-view generative model. We introduce a new satellite-conditioned multi-view diffusion model that combines conditioning from a satellite image and street-level input images when generating novel-views. Our cross-attention based conditioning allows us to retain pretrained multi-view diffusion priors to generate seamless, realistic, novel views from a handful of source views, while also adhering to the scene layout as seen from satellite images. Our model does not use 3D scene reconstruction, and only relies on posed street and satellite views. Our experiments on outdoor datasets show that our model is better than both satellite-conditioned or street-view conditioned baselines on novel-view synthesis, especially on challenging cases where target views have very little overlap with source views. Project page: https://nianticspatial.github.io/groundcontrol

## 9. PDRVLA: A Vision-Language-Action Model with Physical Dynamics Representations（9 分）

- **作者**：Hanqing Wang, Zhaotian Cai, Ruichuan An, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37381v1
- **分类**：cs.RO、cs.CV、cs.LG
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）
- **摘要**：Vision--Language--Action (VLA) models generate robot actions from visual observations and language instructions, but their understanding of physical dynamics remains limited. Video world models can capture scene dynamics, yet generating future videos introduces high latency into the control loop. We introduce PDRVLA, whose lightweight Physical Dynamics Module extracts physical tokens from vision--language features to condition action generation without online video generation. The module learns through direct prediction of future RGB, depth, and optical-flow features, with auxiliary supervision through a frozen pretrained video world model. Training relies entirely on publicly available data, expanded through human-to-robot synthesis, subtask annotation, and embodied chain-of-thought (ECoT) annotation. Across four simulation benchmarks, PDRVLA achieves success rates of 99.3% on LIBERO, 89.9% on LIBERO-Plus, 93.6% on RoboTwin~2.0, and 81.5% on SimplerEnv. Ablations show improved future prediction with world-model supervision and evaluate how subtask conditioning, future object-pose supervision, and ECoT training affect action success.

## 10. TriDifNet: A Unified Three-Task Diffusion Network for End-to-End RGB-T Perception（9 分）

- **作者**：Qianchao He, Jing Liu, Yunhao Li, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.37097v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；detection（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Infrared--visible (IR--VIS) multi-modal perception supports robust scene understanding under challenging illumination. Infrared and visible images are often misaligned due to platform motion, viewpoint changes, and modality-dependent distortions, causing registration errors to propagate into subsequent fusion and detection stages. We propose TriDifNet, a unified three-task diffusion network for end-to-end RGB-T perception. To the best of our knowledge, TriDifNet is the first diffusion-based network that jointly optimizes registration, fusion, and detection for misaligned RGB-T pedestrian perception. TriDifNet consists of three diffusion-based modules, i.e., the Registration Diffusion Module (RegDM), the Fusion Diffusion Module (FusDM), and the Detection Diffusion Module (DetDM). RegDM aligns the unaligned infrared image to the optical reference by estimating a dense deformation field. FusDM performs residual conditional diffusion to generate a detection-oriented fused representation with optical structural cues and complementary infrared saliency. DetDM formulates pedestrian detection as an iterative denoising process in bounding-box space. To couple the three tasks, TriDifNet introduces a closed-loop optimization mechanism, where detection semantics guide fusion and registration, and the fusion result further regularizes geometric alignment. A stage-wise progressive training strategy and a lightweight Nash multi-task learning (Nash-MTL)-based late-stage refinement are further adopted to balance registration, fusion, and detection optimization. Extensive experiments on the KAIST multispectral pedestrian benchmark show that TriDifNet achieves superior downstream detection performance under misaligned RGB-T settings. Against strong system-level competitors, TriDifNet achieves 65.44\% Recall, 57.55\% mAP$_{50}$, and 26.83\% mAP$_{50:95}$.
