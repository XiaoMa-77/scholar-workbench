## 1. MoG-VLM: Efficient Video Anomaly Detection through Motion-Guided Vision-Language Models（16 分）

- **作者**：Khaled Waleed Dawoud, Muhammad Zaigham Zaheer, Khan Muhammad, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31478v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI、cs.LG
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）；detection（标题 ×1）；detection（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Vision-Language Models (VLMs) have shown strong potential for video anomaly detection, but dense frame-level inference makes them impractical for real-time deployment. Existing acceleration commonly relies on uniform sampling, which ignores content dynamics and can either waste computation on static segments or miss localized anomalies. We propose MoG-VLM a motion-adaptive anomaly detection framework that leverages motion purely as a lightweight control signal to decide when VLM inference is necessary. To this end, we introduce Temporal Gradient Magnitude (TGM) which performs content-aware sampling to concentrate computation on informative regions while avoiding redundant processing. We further introduce a hierarchical dual-stream design in which dense multi-frame VLM reasoning is activated only around frames with elevated anomaly likelihood, via anchor-triggered context-window expansion with early termination. A fusion module then reconciles these instantaneous semantic cues with temporal action dynamics. Across four benchmarks, our method achieves 91.91%, 84.30%, 87.88%, and 78.96% AUC-ROC on XD-Violence, UCF-Crime, MSAD, and ShanghaiTech, respectively, establishing new state of the art on three benchmarks under the zero-shot, training-free setting. On XD-Violence, we improve over the previous best result by +0.57% while delivering a 1.31x inference speedup. These results demonstrate that content-aware allocation of VLM capacity, processing fewer frames more intelligently rather than all frames uniformly, is a more effective axis of progress than uniformly densifying VLM inference across frames. Code is available on GitHub: https://github.com/kdawoud91/MoG-VLM.

## 2. Query-Time Calibration of CLIP Features for Open-Vocabulary 3D Segmentation（15 分）

- **作者**：Dominik Engel, Yousef Rajeh, Leon Sick, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.30908v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI
- **命中**：vision-language（摘要 ×3）；segmentation（标题 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）；3D（标题 ×1）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Lifting vision-language features such as CLIP into 3D Gaussian reconstructions enables open-vocabulary segmentation through natural-language queries. Although CLIP represents a broad range of semantic concepts, the lifted features of individual scenes can exhibit strong directional concentration, while raw cosine similarity does not account for the scene-specific feature distribution. We show that substantial segmentation gains can be obtained by changing how a fixed lifted feature field is compared with text. Our method partially subtracts a shared scene direction from visual features and text prototypes and whitens text prototypes using a fixed reference vocabulary of generic terms. It then uses soft clustering for multiclass prediction and CSLS with a relevance margin against the reference vocabulary for single-query prediction. Beyond reducing directional concentration, scene centering improves class-relative feature separation in every evaluated scene. Across seven lifting methods and four datasets, the resulting query-time calibration consistently improves mean intersection over union in both multiclass and single-query segmentation. These gains require no retraining, source-image re-encoding, or feature re-lifting, demonstrating the value of scene-conditioned comparison for making better use of existing lifted representations.

## 3. GeoMem-VLA: Internalizing 3D Scene Understanding into Temporal Memory for Vision-Language-Action Models（13 分）

- **作者**：Qinfan Zhang, Yifan Luo, Haoyuan Shi, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.29866v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；3D（标题 ×1）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Geometric structure is essential for robotic manipulation and understanding the 3D world. Most VLA models rely on 2D representations of current observations, while geometry-aware approaches often require additional 3D information. However, geometry inferred independently from individual observations is often discarded rather than accumulated into a persistent task representation. Our key insight is that action generation needs a compact, task-relevant geometric abstraction that can be continuously updated, rather than a complete, renderable 3D reconstruction at every step. We introduce GeoMem-VLA, a VLA framework that internalizes explicit 3D supervision into dedicated geometric tokens and maintains them as temporal memory for action generation, without directly aligning the VLM's native visual embeddings with the teacher feature space. During training, a frozen geometry foundation model supervises learnable GeoPlan tokens through a geometric flow-matching objective over the current scene point map and future end-effector tracks. These tokens are integrated with the memory of observation representations, where historical information is retrieved, adaptively fused with current observations, and consolidated to retain event frames as the task progresses. At inference, the policy retrieves historical representations and fuses them with the current working representation to predict actions, without invoking the 3D teacher or explicitly reconstructing the scene. After benchmark-specific downstream adaptation, GeoMem-VLA achieves 98.9% average success on LIBERO and 91.10% / 91.24% on RoboTwin's clean/randomized settings. Across five real-world tasks, GeoMem-VLA achieves 78.0% average success. These results suggest that internalizing and remembering 3D scene understanding is a promising approach to robot control.

## 4. CellTrack-Bench: Microscopic Cell Tracking Benchmark for Vision-Language Models（12 分）

- **作者**：Ilan Theodoro, Jordão Bragantini, Loïc A. Royer
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31566v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Large language models have shown outstanding performance on text-first tasks such as coding, math, and writing. Recent efforts are shifting models' training to include scientific problems that often live in highly specialized domains beyond text, such as protein structure and biological images. However, in these scenarios, their general-purpose accuracy compared with specialized algorithms remains in question. One such domain is cell tracking, which requires spatiotemporal awareness of cell dynamics. To evaluate this, we created a new benchmark, CellTrack-Bench, which assesses Vision-Language Models' (VLMs) cell-tracking-related performance against specialized trackers. The full benchmark draws from 15 standard cell-tracking datasets across four imaging modalities and comprises 1,537 problems corresponding to 9,041 cell-linking decisions. We selected a hard subset requiring greater reasoning effort by identifying failures shared across distinct algorithmic trackers. With this proposed dataset, we: (i) evaluated different environment harnesses for VLMs; (ii) benchmarked 9 frontier closed-source models and 13 open-weight models; and (iii) provided a detailed analysis of their failure modes. Code, the stored model outputs, and a pipeline that regenerates the benchmark from the source datasets are available at https://github.com/royerlab/celltrack-bench.

## 5. Diffusion-Based Synthesis of Complete 3D Biparametric Prostate MRI（12 分）

- **作者**：Claudia Giardina, Montse Pardàs, Verónica Vilaplana
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.31523v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；3D（标题 ×1）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Synthetic biparametric MRI (bpMRI) may help alleviate the limited availability of large and representative prostate MRI datasets for deep learning applications. In this work, we present a conditional 3D diffusion framework for anatomy-conditioned synthesis of complete prostate bpMRI from anatomical masks. The framework supports sequential synthesis, in which T2w and diffusion modalities are generated by separately trained models, and joint synthesis, in which all bpMRI modalities are generated simultaneously within a single model. Five configurations were trained using PI-CAI, FastMRI Prostate, and PROSTATEx datasets to investigate different conditioning schemes, target modalities, and synthesis strategies. Evaluation using distribution-based metrics, paired-image similarity, and qualitative assessment demonstrated the feasibility of both synthesis pathways. Joint synthesis achieved competitive distributional fidelity while generating complete bpMRI, whereas direct synthesis of ADC and computed high-b-value DWI outperformed their derivation from synthetic diffusion-weighted images.
