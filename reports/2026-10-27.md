## 1. OVM-Occ: Open-Vocabulary Monocular Occupancy Prediction via Geometry-Aware Vision-Language Distillation（13 分）

- **作者**：Shuyang Yu, Haibo Qin, Shaohan Hu, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35312v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Monocular occupancy prediction in indoor scenes aims to recover a scene's 3D geometry and semantics from a single RGB image. Existing methods are restricted to a closed set of predefined categories, limiting their ability to generalize to unseen categories. This limitation is particularly critical in indoor scenes, where objects exhibit highly diverse and fine-grained semantic categories. We study open-vocabulary monocular occupancy prediction for indoor scenes and present OVM-Occ, a Gaussian-based occupancy framework with an instance-aware vision-language teacher. Specifically, we adopt Language-Embedded Gaussians as the intermediate scene representation, propose a geometry-aware deformable attention module to refine Gaussian semantic features, and construct instance-level vision-language embeddings to provide reliable semantic supervision. We further introduce geometry-aware decoupled semantic distillation to mitigate the interference between geometry learning and semantic learning. To evaluate open-vocabulary occupancy prediction performance, we introduce OpenOcc-ScanNet and OpenOcc-Replica, two extended benchmarks with more diverse and fine-grained semantic categories. Experiments show that our method achieves state-of-the-art performance in open-vocabulary monocular occupancy prediction for indoor scenes, outperforming the previous state-of-the-art approach by 12.2 mIoU and 0.6 IoU on the Occ-ScanNet dataset, with consistent gains under new open-vocabulary evaluation protocols.

## 2. MemVLN: Streaming Episodic Memory for Vision-Language Navigation（13 分）

- **作者**：Yan Wang, Jiangyong Huang, Baoxiong Jia, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35066v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Vision-language navigation (VLN) requires an embodied agent to follow language instructions through 3D environments. Maintaining persistent episodic memory remains a challenge for VLM-based streaming agents in long-horizon navigation. We present MEMVLN, a streaming episodic memory framework that maintains a bounded bank of compressed perceptual features annotated with timesteps and action-derived relative poses, and produces compact memory tokens via cross-attention. We pair the proposed memory with a frozen pretrained geometry encoder. To learn the memory module, we design a masking-based trajectory summarization task with multi-granularity sub-trajectory decomposition, encouraging route reconstruction exclusively from episodic memory. Under navigation training restricted to the official R2R/RxR trajectories, MEMVLN achieves state-of-the-art performance on most reported metrics on standard VLN-CE benchmarks. Long-horizon analyses further characterize when episodic memory is useful. For physical evaluation, we collect 30 instruction-conditioned episodes in one physical scene, use 24 as demonstrations for post-training, and hold out six for testing. On the held-out episodes, MEMVLN improves success over the VLM baseline from 17% to 40%, and post-training further raises it to 47%.

## 3. VidEdit3D: Flexible 3D Gaussian Editing with Video Diffusion（12 分）

- **作者**：Yanming Yang, Chenxi Song, Tong Zhao, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35756v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；3D（标题 ×1）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Recent advancements in text-guided 3D editing, particularly with 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS), have shown promise but still lag behind the flexibility of 2D editing tools. Existing methods face a critical trade-off between preserving multi-view consistency and allowing structural modifications. Image-based editors can also struggle to apply the intended edit to objects viewed from unusual angles. To address these limitations, we introduce VidEdit3D, a framework that reformulates 3D scene editing as a trajectory-based video editing task. By drawing a parallel between camera movement in static 3D scenes and temporal dynamics in video, we leverage the robust spatiotemporal priors of pre-trained Video Diffusion Models (VDMs) to encourage semantic and structural coherence across views. Specifically, we propose Trajectory-Guided Rendering (TGR) to select informative keyframes and construct smooth video clips aligned with VDM input requirements. To propagate edits from an anchor view to these clips, we develop Flow-Aligned Video Editing (FAVE), a training-free framework incorporating an Editing Consistency Guidance (ECG) mechanism. ECG contrasts source and edited image conditions at a shared latent state, using scheduled source-image amplification to strengthen adherence to the edited reference while maintaining original scene context. Finally, we introduce Region-Selective 3DGS Refinement (RS-GS) to strictly confine geometric and appearance updates to target primitives, reducing background attribute leakage. Experiments demonstrate stronger instruction alignment and better multi-view consistency than the baselines.

## 4. Generative Structure from Motion with Native 3D Diffusion（12 分）

- **作者**：Haobin Duan, Binbin Huang, Mochu Xiang, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35522v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；3D（标题 ×1）；3D（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Recovering an object's 3D structure and camera motion from a few uncalibrated views is a fundamental problem in computer vision. Classical structure from motion methods recover 3D geometry consistent with the input images but cannot recover unseen regions; native generation methods produce plausible 3D objects which are related but not necessarily faithful to the input images. Methods simply concatenating reconstruction and generation allow error to cascade across the two stages, instead of producing a 3D object consistent with both the distribution and the input. We propose GenSfM, which integrates image-based 3D reconstruction with a native 3D diffusion model. In particular, we use a multi-modal diffusion transformer to jointly denoise the shape latent and per-view pose latents under shared self-attention, grounding generated geometry in the observed views while using object-level priors to infer unobserved regions. A compact 3D-native UV-volume tokenization keeps the joint sequence small as views are added, and a pose-aware stage refines geometry and texture by sampling per-voxel image features at the recovered camera poses. On Toys4k and GSO, for $4$ uncalibrated views, GenSfM improves novel-view PSNR by $3.2$-$4.5$ dB and reduces median Chamfer distance by $2.8$-$3.8\times$ over a strong native-3D generation baseline, while recovering input-view poses over $96\%$ Acc@$5^\circ$ without any external pose-estimation backbone, post-hoc optimization, or known intrinsics. Project page: https://dotb1ue.github.io/GenSfm/.

## 5. Beyond Vision-Language Models: Visual-Semantic Affect Graphs for Emotion Recognition in Artworks（12 分）

- **作者**：Ngoc-Duy Mai, Ha-Thanh Nguyen, Hideaki Takeda
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35244v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.AI、cs.CL、cs.LG
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Emotion recognition in artworks remains challenging because affective meaning is often subjective, implicit, and shaped by relationships between visual form and semantic associations. While vision-language models provide strong multimodal representations, direct image-to-emotion prediction may overlook explicit relations among formal, semantic, and affective cues that support emotional interpretation. This paper proposes Visual-Semantic Affect Graphs (VSAG), a graph-based framework that represents artworks through interpretable feature nodes, including color, brushstroke, composition, light-shadow, line quality, and extracted visual entities. The graph learns feature-emotion associations for dominant emotion prediction, with valence-arousal structure modeled through an optional hierarchical auxiliary affect module. We evaluate VSAG and VSAG-TopK against direct VLM inference using Qwen3-VL and InternVL3.5 on EmoArt-5k. Results show that direct VLMs provide useful but modest baselines, while graph-based models improve dominant emotion prediction. In particular, the hierarchical valence-arousal variant of VSAG achieves the best overall performance, improving accuracy, macro-F1, and weighted-F1 over both direct VLM inference and the emotion-only VSAG baseline. Per-class results indicate that graph-based models and VLMs capture partly complementary signals, while valence-arousal structure is most useful when integrated as a coarse-to-fine affect hierarchy rather than as a simple post-hoc correction. These findings suggest that explicit visual-semantic structure can support both prediction and interpretation in artwork emotion recognition.

## 6. Good Rankings, Poor Choices? Rethinking Language-Only Vision-Language Model Selection（12 分）

- **作者**：Hyuk Kwon, Jaebyeong Jeon, Hyeonseo Jang, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.34685v1
- **分类**：cs.CV、cs.LG
- **命中**：vision-language（标题 ×3）；vision-language（摘要 ×3）
- **摘要**：Language-Only Vision-Language Model Selection (LOVM) aims to identify the Vision-Language Model (VLM) with the highest zero-shot accuracy on a target task using only textual information. We revisit the existing LOVM evaluation protocol and argue that ranking metrics, such as top-5 recall and Kendall's rank correlation, do not directly assess the performance of the selected model. We therefore adopt simple regret (SR), defined as the performance gap between the best and selected models, and introduce Outlier-Robust Normalized Regret (ORNR) to account for the varying performance ranges across tasks while reducing sensitivity to outliers. Building on this revised evaluation protocol, we propose Target-Conditioned Expected Source Regret (TC-ESR), a training-free method that selects the model with the lowest similarity-weighted mean regret across source tasks. Across 22 target tasks and 43 VLMs, TC-ESR achieves the lowest mean SR and ORNR among all compared methods. Separately, we identify two sources of target performance leakage in the prior ranking-based evaluation protocol and provide corrections that remove both.

## 7. S$^2$-RL: Sample-Set Dual Reinforcement Learning for Generative Semantic Segmentation Dataset Distillation（10 分）

- **作者**：Haoyu Wang, Fei Zhou, Qingqing Qiu, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35491v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（摘要 ×2）；segmentation（标题 ×2）；segmentation（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：While dataset distillation has witnessed significant advances in image classification, its extension to dense prediction tasks such as semantic segmentation is plagued by two core bottlenecks: bi-level optimization-based approaches suffer from prohibitive computational costs stemming from pixel-wise gradient unrolling, whereas proxy-based generative methods merely infuse visual textures into fixed semantic masks derived from real data, thus failing to condense rich semantic knowledge into a compact set of informative, novel scene generations. To address both bottlenecks simultaneously, we propose a novel Sample-Set Reinforcement Learning (S$^2$-RL) framework for generative semantic segmentation dataset distillation (SSDD). Specifically, S$^2$-RL formulates SSDD as a diffusion model based text-to-image generation paradigm, enabling flexible generation of images with informative, novel semantic distributions via a concise text prompt. Furthermore, we fine-tune a diffusion policy via Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO), leveraging a sample-set dual reward paradigm: for individual samples, it enforces semantic alignment with input text prompts and intra-class feature diversity; for sample sets, it maximizes inter-sample semantic distribution diversity by solving a contextual multi-armed bandit problem. These design choices enable S$^2$-RL to distill large-scale semantic segmentation datasets into a compact set of generated samples that encapsulate diverse, informative semantic knowledge without the need of pixel-wise gradient unrolling. Extensive experiments on the ADE20K and COCO datasets demonstrate that S$^2$-RL achieves substantial and consistent improvements over state-of-the-art baselines, establishing a new benchmark for SSDD.

## 8. Domain-Adapted Latent Diffusion Models for CT-to-PET Image Translation（9 分）

- **作者**：Yann Bourdé, Lea Bogensperger, Arnela Hadzic, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35132v1
- **分类**：cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）；detection（摘要 ×1）
- **摘要**：Positron Emission Tomography (PET) provides important metabolic information for tumor detection and staging but remains significantly less accessible than Computed Tomography (CT) due to its high cost, infrastructure requirements, and reliance on injected radioactive tracers. CT-to-PET image translation using generative models aims to address this disparity, yet state-of-the-art diffusion-based methods are limited by the scarcity of paired training data. To overcome this, we propose a domain-adapted latent diffusion framework that transfers natural-image priors using a relatively small dataset of 21,930 paired CT/PET slices, incorporating a signed power transform to resolve extreme intensity sparsity in PET data. Compared to CPDM, a state-ofthe-art baseline for CT-to-PET image translation, our approach yields superior performance in pixel-wise reconstruction and localized tumor fidelity, demonstrating effective transfer of natural-image priors to the medical domain. We further provide a systematic analysis of evaluation metric reliability for sparse PET data, showing that standard full-image metrics can be misleading indicators of translated image quality, and propose background-filtered and mask-restricted metrics as more clinically meaningful alternatives.

## 9. Learning When to Commit: Schedule Distillation for Fast Diffusion Language Models（8 分）

- **作者**：Aidar Myrzakhan, Zhiqiang Shen
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35804v1
- **分类**：cs.CL、cs.AI、cs.LG
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：Diffusion large language models (dLLMs) can generate multiple tokens in parallel, but often require many decoding rounds to complete a response. Efficient generation depends not only on which tokens are easy to predict, but also on how committing to them helps predict the remaining positions. A highly predictable token may provide little useful context, while revealing a less predictable token can help several other positions become predictable sooner. We propose dSpark, a new schedule distillation-based framework for learning when to commit tokens for faster diffusion language generation. To achieve this, we construct unmasking schedules that prioritize tokens whose reveal makes other positions easier to predict, reducing uncertainty earlier in decoding. We then distill these schedules into the model to enable faster parallel sampling, with a KL-divergence regularizer to help preserve generation quality. We extensively evaluate our approach across mathematical reasoning and code generation tasks. On GSM8K, our method achieves remarkable 10.12$\times$ and 5.12$\times$ speedups over the various baselines on LLaDA-8B-Instruct and Dream-7B-Instruct, respectively, while maintaining superior task performance. Our code is available at https://github.com/VILA-Lab/dSpark.

## 10. Triage: Skip, Skim, or Refine Attention Tiles in Video Diffusion Transformers（8 分）

- **作者**：Yeonjae Kim, Omin Kwon, Seong Hoon Seo, et al.
- **链接**：http://arxiv.org/abs/2610.35706v1
- **分类**：cs.LG、cs.CV
- **命中**：diffusion（标题 ×2）；diffusion（摘要 ×2）
- **摘要**：Attention over tens of thousands of visual tokens dominates the inference of video diffusion transformers (DiTs). Existing methods reduce this cost through sparsity, low precision, or both, but use a single precision for all kept tiles. We observe that the output error introduced by approximation varies widely across tiles. Many tiles can be skipped with little error, others must be computed but gain little beyond low precision, and a few retain substantial error even at low precision. We present Triage, a training-free attention method that assigns each tile one of three approximation tiers: skipping it by replacing it with its block means, skimming it in NVFP4, or refining it with NVFP4 residual correction. A lightweight router predicts each tile's error under each tier from block statistics and moves a tile to a more accurate tier only when the predicted error reduction justifies the extra compute, with a single price setting this trade-off. A fused kernel executes all three tiers in a single pass on the FP4 tensor cores of RTX Blackwell GPUs. Across Wan2.2, HunyuanVideo, and LTX-2.5, Triage accelerates end-to-end generation by up to 2.32x over dense FlashAttention, and in each of five settings it runs faster than every baseline while reaching 0.3-3.0 dB higher PSNR than the highest-PSNR baseline.
